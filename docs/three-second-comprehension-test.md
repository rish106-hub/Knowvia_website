# Three-second comprehension test

Use the production homepage at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Show the first viewport to five people who have not previously seen Knowvia for three seconds, then hide it.

Ask only: “What does this product help someone do?” Do not prompt with insurance, policy, family, known, unknown, or next.

A response passes when it identifies both:

1. understanding existing insurance cover before or during a high-pressure health event; and
2. at least two of known facts, unclear or conflicting information, and next actions.
